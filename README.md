# Hi, I'm Luis Carretero 👋

### Telecommunications Engineer · RF & UWB · Embedded Systems · Signal Processing

I'm a Telecommunications Systems Engineering graduate from the **Universidad Politécnica de Cartagena (UPCT)**, currently pursuing a **Master's in Telecommunications Engineering** at the same university.

My main interests are RF and microwave engineering, antenna arrays, FPGA development, and digital signal processing. I also build applications and explore how AI coding agents can support engineering work, from simulation and testing to hardware design.

## What I'm working on

- 📡 **UWB radar and beamforming** — Developing simulations for antenna arrays, MUSIC direction-of-arrival estimation, True-Time-Delay beamforming, and range–Doppler analysis as part of my final degree project.
- 📱 **UWBLab** — Building an experimental iOS app with SwiftUI and Nearby Interaction to explore UWB ranging, direction finding, and proximity interactions. External UWB hardware is the next stage.
- 🛰️ **SAR image processing** — Studying contrast enhancement, noise reduction, interpolation, and spatial and frequency filtering of MSTAR radar images in MATLAB.
- 🛠️ **RF hardware design** — Exploring KiCad, circuit simulation, and RF analysis tools for future UWB boards and antenna experiments.
- 🤖 **Applications and AI agents** — Building web applications and experimenting with agent workflows for implementation, review, and verification.
